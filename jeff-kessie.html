Meet Jeff

Founder, Chief Executive Officer, and Chief Information Officer

CONNECT WITH JEFF

Jeff Kessie serves as Founder of Cloud Commercial Holdings LLC, where he drives innovation strategy, cloud transformation, and secure infrastructure services. Jeff is committed to delivering full life cycle solutions that enable organizations to maximize the value of their technology investments while maintaining the highest standards of security and performance.

Prior to founding Cloud Commercial Holdings, Jeff served at Booz Allen Hamilton, where he led a major cloud migration initiative, transitioning more than 100 legacy systems to modern cloud platforms while implementing a Zero Trust security architecture. He also held a key role at Amazon Web Services (AWS), where he developed automation solutions leveraging tools such as AWS CloudFormation and Pulumi to accelerate and streamline service delivery.

Jeff holds Department of Defense IAT Level III certifications and brings extensive expertise in cloud architecture, automation, and secure systems design for both public and private sector clients. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from George Mason University.
