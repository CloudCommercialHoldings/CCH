Meet Kunandy

Systems Engineer

CONNECT WITH KUNANDY

Kunandy Ouattara serves as a Systems Engineer at Cloud Commercial Holdings LLC, where he designs, automates, and secures the cloud and AI systems behind the company's services. Kunandy brings a builder's mindset to every engagement, turning complex requirements into reliable, well-documented solutions that teams can trust and scale.

Kunandy's past projects include building AI agents on brand-new software to automate time-consuming work. He contributed across the full life of that project, from setting up and securing the environment to testing the solution and presenting the results to stakeholders.

Kunandy also founded Stratus Lab, an online training platform that helps professionals earn cloud certifications and grow their careers. He built the platform from the ground up, and his hands-on experience in Amazon Web Services (AWS) includes designing and deploying cloud solutions.

Kunandy holds a security clearance and is cloud certified, with an academic foundation in information systems and cybersecurity. He is pursuing a Master of Business Administration with a concentration in Technology Management at Longwood University.
