Meet Kunandy

Systems Engineer

CONNECT WITH KUNANDY

Kunandy Ouattara serves as a Systems Engineer at Cloud Commercial Holdings LLC, where he supports cloud infrastructure, automation, and AI initiatives. Kunandy is focused on building secure, reliable systems that help organizations get the most value from their technology.

Kunandy built Stratus Lab, a cloud certification training platform that offers AWS, Azure, and multi-cloud course tracks alongside resume and interview coaching, with integrated online payments and scheduling for live, hands-on sessions. He has designed and deployed a range of solutions in Amazon Web Services (AWS) across compute, storage, networking, and identity. During his time at Plus3 IT, he built a multi-agent AI pipeline on NVIDIA NemoClaw that automates Department of Defense SBIR solicitation research using orchestrator, collector, processor, and validator agents.

Kunandy holds a security clearance and the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification. He is pursuing a Master of Business Administration with a concentration in Technology Management at Longwood University, building on an academic background in business, information systems, and cybersecurity.
